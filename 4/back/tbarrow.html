T-Bar Row

How to Perform

Stand over the bar with your feet about shoulder-width apart. Bend at your hips and knees while keeping your back neutral, then grip the handles firmly.

Pull the weight toward your torso by driving your elbows back. Keep your chest controlled and your spine neutral throughout the movement, then lower the weight slowly until your arms are extended.

Primary Muscles

Latissimus dorsi (Lats)

Secondary Muscles

Rhomboid major (Rhomboids), trapezius (Traps), posterior deltoid (Rear Delt), and biceps brachii (Biceps)
