Pull-Up

How to Perform

Grip the pull-up bar slightly wider than shoulder-width apart with your palms facing away from you. Hang from the bar with your arms extended and your core engaged.

Pull your body upward by driving your elbows down toward your sides. Continue until your chin clears the bar, then lower yourself under control until your arms are extended again.

Primary Muscles

Latissimus dorsi (Lats)

Secondary Muscles

Biceps brachii (Biceps), trapezius (Traps), and posterior deltoid (Rear Delt)
