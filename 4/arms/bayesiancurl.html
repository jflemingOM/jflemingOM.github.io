Bayesian Curl

How to Perform

Stand in front of a low cable pulley with your working arm behind your body. Grip the handle with your palm facing upward and keep your upper arm slightly behind your torso.

Curl the handle forward and upward while keeping your upper arm behind your body. Squeeze your biceps at the top, then slowly extend your elbow back to the starting position.

Primary Muscles

Biceps brachii (Biceps)

Secondary Muscles

Brachialis (Biceps) and brachioradialis (Forearms)
