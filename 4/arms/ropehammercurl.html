Rope Hammer Curl

How to Perform

Stand facing a low cable pulley and grip the rope with a neutral, palms-facing-inward grip. Keep your elbows close to your sides and your torso stable.

Curl the rope upward by bending your elbows while keeping your upper arms stationary. Squeeze your arms at the top, then lower the rope slowly to the starting position.

Primary Muscles

Brachialis (Biceps)

Secondary Muscles

Biceps brachii (Biceps) and brachioradialis (Forearms)
