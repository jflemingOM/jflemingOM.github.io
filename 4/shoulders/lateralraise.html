Lateral Raise

How to Perform

Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart and hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and maintain a controlled posture.

Raise your arms out to the sides until they are about parallel with the floor. Pause briefly, then lower the dumbbells slowly back to your sides.

Primary Muscles

Medial deltoid (Side Delt)

Secondary Muscles

Anterior deltoid (Front Delt) and posterior deltoid (Rear Delt)
