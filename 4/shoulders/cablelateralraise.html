Cable Lateral Raise

How to Perform

Stand beside a low cable pulley and hold the handle with the hand farthest from the machine. Keep your arm slightly bent and your torso stable.

Raise your arm out to the side until it is about parallel with the floor. Lower the handle slowly and maintain control throughout the movement.

Primary Muscles

Medial deltoid (Side Delt)

Secondary Muscles

Anterior deltoid (Front Delt) and posterior deltoid (Rear Delt)
