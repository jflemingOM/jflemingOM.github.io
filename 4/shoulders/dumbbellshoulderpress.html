Dumbbell Shoulder Press

How to Perform

Sit or stand with a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height. Keep your core braced and your wrists stacked over your elbows.

Press both dumbbells overhead until your arms are extended. Lower the dumbbells under control back to shoulder height while maintaining a stable torso.

Primary Muscles

Anterior deltoid (Front Delt) and medial deltoid (Side Delt)

Secondary Muscles

Triceps brachii (Triceps), upper trapezius (Upper Traps), and pectoralis major (Upper Chest)
