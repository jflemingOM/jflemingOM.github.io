Leg Extension

How to Perform

Sit in the leg extension machine with your back against the pad and your knees aligned with the machine's pivot point. Position the lower pad against the front of your lower legs.

Extend your knees to raise the pad while keeping your hips and back against the seat. Squeeze your quadriceps at the top, then lower the weight slowly back to the starting position.

Primary Muscles

Quadriceps femoris (Quads)

Secondary Muscles

Tensor fasciae latae (Hip Flexor)
