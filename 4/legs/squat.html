Squat

How to Perform

Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart with the barbell resting across your upper back. Brace your core and keep your chest controlled as you prepare to squat.

Bend your knees and hips to lower your body while keeping your feet planted on the floor. Descend to a comfortable depth, then drive through the floor to return to the starting position.

Primary Muscles

Quadriceps femoris (Quads) and gluteus maximus (Glutes)

Secondary Muscles

Hamstrings (Hamstrings), adductor magnus (Adductors), and erector spinae (Lower Back)
