Incline Dumbbell Bench Press

How to Perform

Set the bench to a moderate incline and sit with a dumbbell in each hand. Position the dumbbells near your shoulders and keep your feet planted firmly on the floor.

Press both dumbbells upward while keeping them controlled and aligned over your chest. Lower them slowly toward the sides of your upper chest before pressing them back up.

Primary Muscles

Pectoralis major (Upper Chest)

Secondary Muscles

Anterior deltoid (Front Delt) and triceps brachii (Triceps)
