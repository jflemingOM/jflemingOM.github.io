Cable Chest Fly

How to Perform

Set the cable pulleys around chest height and grasp one handle in each hand. Step forward into a stable stance and keep a slight bend in your elbows.

Bring your hands together in front of your chest in a controlled arc while keeping your elbows slightly bent. Slowly return your arms to the starting position while maintaining tension in your chest.

Primary Muscles

Pectoralis major (Chest)

Secondary Muscles

Anterior deltoid (Front Delt)
