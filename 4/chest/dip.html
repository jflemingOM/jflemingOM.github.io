Dip

How to Perform

Grip the parallel bars firmly and support your body with your arms extended. Lean your torso slightly forward while keeping your feet behind you.

Lower your body under control by bending your elbows and allowing your chest to move between the bars. Press through your hands to return to the starting position while keeping your movement controlled.

Primary Muscles

Pectoralis major (Chest)

Secondary Muscles

Triceps brachii (Triceps) and anterior deltoid (Front Delt)
